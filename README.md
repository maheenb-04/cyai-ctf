CYAI's fall Capture The Flag at York College.

The Rogue AI Recovery is a beginner-friendly CTF run by the York College Cybersecurity & AI Club. It starts
with a two-hour session in the lab, and the challenges stay open for 24 hours after that. Teams of up to four work through
challenges in web, OSINT, cryptography, forensics, reverse engineering, prompt injection, AI safety, social engineering,
finance and aviation, and submit flags in the format `CYAI{...}`. Everything runs in the browser, so a phone or laptop is all you need.

## How to play

1. Register on the site with a username, email and password.
2. Create a team, or join one with the team name and password from a teammate.
3. Challenges open at 12:00 PM Eastern on October 29 and close at 12:00 PM on October 30.
4. Solve a challenge, submit the flag, and check the scoreboard.
5. The scoreboard freezes at 2:00 PM on October 29. Those standings decide the prizes. Flags you capture after that
   still show on your team page.

## Rules

- Don't share flags with other teams.
- Don't brute-force flag submissions. Submissions are rate limited.
- One account per person. Use your real name so we can verify the winners.
- If you're stuck, ask an organizer.
